Story line

3 Idiots is a story about three engineering students and their college life. The movie focuses on friendship, pressure of studies, career expectations, and following one’s passion. Rancho believes in learning for knowledge, not just for marks, and inspires his friends to think differently.

Review

3 Idiots is an inspirational and entertaining movie with a strong message.
